How to play

  1. Bridge — one word fits both clues in order. BEE between HONEY and HIVE makes honeybee and beehive.
  2. Shift — two meanings of the same word. BARK connects TREE and DOG.
  3. Relay — fill two gaps left to right. Between CHEESE and TAPE, CAKE and MIX make cheesecake, cake mix, and mixtape.

Three guesses per missing word. A new puzzle arrives at midnight UTC, shared worldwide. Progress is saved in your browser only — no account or download needed.
