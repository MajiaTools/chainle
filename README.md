# Chainle

**Find the missing links.**

Chainle is a free, independent browser word puzzle. Each daily puzzle has four missing words in three rounds. Find the words that connect the clues — some links form compound words, while others connect two meanings of the same word.

Play it here: https://chainle.app/

## How to play

1. **Bridge** — type one word that fits both clues in order. **BEE** between HONEY and HIVE makes *honeybee* and *beehive*.
2. **Shift** — use two meanings of the same word. **BARK** connects TREE and DOG because it is a tree's outer layer and the sound a dog makes.
3. **Relay** — fill two gaps from left to right. Between CHEESE and TAPE, **CAKE** and **MIX** make *cheesecake*, *cake mix*, and *mixtape*.
4. You get three guesses per missing word. Solve all four missing words to complete the puzzle.

## Daily and unlimited

- A new puzzle arrives at **midnight UTC** and is the same for everyone on that UTC date.
- Open the daily puzzle archive to replay an older edition, or try unlimited word chain puzzles for a new mix right away.
- Progress is saved in your browser only — no account, payment or download is needed.
